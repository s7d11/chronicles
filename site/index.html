From Phantom Parses to Live 13-Language Model Efficiency Across Mali’s Tongues

Yesterday’s Sebeni overhaul eliminated up to 65.3% phantom grammatical inflation and expanded 13 dictionaries to 49,257 entries. Today, we wire those guarded parsers into a live MALI13 leaderboard pitting Sebeni’s 2B GRPO checkpoint against Gemini 3/3.1, Gemma-4, and 11 frontier open models.

Recently awarded access to Google’s Antigravity for Science by the Antigravity team (Antigravity access only; cloud compute was not provided), this Chronicle serves as an open laboratory notebook for out-of-reach investigations and “What-If” experiments that ordinarily stay on the whiteboard. Roughly 98% of the code, analysis, and broadsheet prose here is generated and verified by Gemini 4 Neon-High-Orchestrator inside Antigravity with minimal human oversight.

For our inaugural edition, we turn yesterday’s deep computational linguistics audit into a living public benchmark. Evaluating Mali’s thirteen national languages (MULTI13: Bambara, Bomu, Bozo, Dogon, Fula, Kassonke, Hassaniya, Maninka, Mamara, Songhay, Soninke, Supyire, and Tamasheq) revealed that legacy automated rewards suffered from up to 65.3% phantom parses—rules that split unglossed words while failing dictionary lookup.

Rebuilding every non-Bambara grammar from raw corpus evidence, scaling the lexicons from 19,018 to 49,257 headwords, and enforcing DabaX(strict_parse=True) lifted median 2023 Constitution parse coverage from 23.39% to 97.59%. Those same strict analyzers now power MALI13, scoring Morphology, Generation Purity, 13-way Language ID, Morphosyntactic Cloze, and Subword Diacritic Penalty.

Instead of static screenshots, the Live Leaderboard pulls directly from reproducible JSON artifacts generated across Adaption Labs, NVIDIA Build (NIM), self-funded Vertex AI endpoints, and a single local RTX 3060 running mlsftwrs/sebeni-gemma-4-e2b-grpo-50.

II. Morphotactics Overhaul

30,239 New Entries & Zero DictReader Errors: Fixed blank-line gluing, uppercase \ps mismatches, copy-pasted grammars, and ~9,900 Tamasheq Cyrillic homoglyphs.

Across stratified 300-line raw samples and the 2023 Constitution of Mali, median true parse coverage jumped from 12.72% → 82.53% (raw) and 23.39% → 97.59% (Constitution), topped by a two-stage Berber clitic & circumfix engine for Tamasheq. Inspect all 13 language dispatches →
