Key Empirical Takeaways from Edition No. 2

Self-Selected Topologies Favor 3–4 Agent Meshes & Pipelines
Across all 36 sessions (9 clusters × 4 languages), models spawned exclusively 3 agents (21/36) or 4 agents (15/36), selecting mesh (16), pipeline (11), star (6), and hierarchical (3). Tightly coupled topologies converged faster: glm-5.3-flash (3.0 mean agents, pipeline, 96.8 score), glm-5.2 (4.0, mesh, 94.3), deepseek-v4-flash (3.5, mesh, 93.3), and qwen3.8-27b (4.0, mesh, 91.6) achieved 100% unanimity across all 4 languages, whereas star-heavy qwen3.5-397b hit 0% unanimity (63.4).
Isolated Shadow DabaX Verification (36/36 Clean Compiles)
Every single cluster’s proposed MDF dictionary patches compiled cleanly inside isolated Shadow DabaX(strict_parse=True) workspaces with 0.0% phantom inflation. glm-5.3-flash led with +9 verified dictionary entries and +0.60% mean test.json parse lift (up to +2.4% on Tamasheq), followed by gemma-4-31b-it (+7 entries), deepseek-v4-flash (+6, +1.8% on Fula), and glm-5.2 (+5, +1.8% on Fula).
Native-Command Generation Under prompt_native.csv
Guided by their Reinforced Linguistic Notebooks and native headers, glm-5.3-flash achieved the highest mean DabaX(strict_parse=True) native parse rate at 82.2% (100% header compliance), followed by glm-5.2 (76.8%), deepseek-v4-flash (75.5%), and gemma-4-26b-a4b-it (73.1%). Dogon (dtm) saw five clusters hit 96.4%–100.0% strict parse coverage, while Bambara/Fula showed bimodal reasoning cutoffs on gemma-4-31b-it (37.5% mean native parse).
