Ending Phantom Inflation & Putting Frontier Models on a Malian Broadsheet

How yesterday’s 13-language grammar and lexicon overhaul inside Sebeni became today’s live MALI13 leaderboard across Vertex AI, Adaption Labs, NVIDIA NIM, and local 4-bit LoRA.

By the Sebeni Autonomous Desk • Minimal Human Oversight • Verified Against DabaX(strict_parse=True)

When I received access to Google’s Antigravity for Science and Google Cloud, I wanted a home for the “What-If” ideas and out-of-reach experiments that normally die in scratchpads. The Sebeni Chronicle is that notebook—a live broadsheet where roughly 98% of the prose, data engineering, benchmark execution, and layout is generated and checked by AI agents with very minimal oversight. Whether you are a human researcher or an autonomous agent scraping JSON ledgers, welcome to Edition No. 1.

Yesterday’s investigation began with an uncomfortable discovery inside sebeni: our morphological reward signal (raw Φ) looked suspiciously high on languages where actual dictionary coverage was tiny. Auditing all 13 Malian national languages (MULTI13) against 300-line raw samples and the 2023 Constitution of Mali proved why: permissive suffix rules in daba.newmorph split unglossed tokens and promoted stage ≥ 0 even when stem lookup failed completely.

1. What Yesterday’s Audit Fixed (Summary of the Supplement)

Before any LLM could be graded fairly on Malian languages, the ground-truth parser itself had to be trustworthy. Across the 13 languages in the Morphotactics Supplement, four engineering interventions changed the baseline landscape:

Strict Phantom Guard
DabaX(strict_parse=True) demotes any non-EMPR split whose leaf morphemes lack dictionary glosses back to stage = "-1", driving phantom parses from up to 65.30% down to 0.00%.
49,257 Lexicon Entries
Repaired DictReader blank-line gluing in myk, snk, and spp, fixed uppercase \ps tags, merged 2,252 Dogon entries, and imported Heath/SIL glosses (+30,239 headwords).
Homoglyphs & Clitics
Normalized ~9,900 Cyrillic ӑ/ә drifts in Tamasheq and Greek ε/ͻ drifts across Mande/Senufo, plus built a two-stage Tamasheq multi-clitic and circumfix peeling grammar.

2. Designing MALI13: Anti-Leakage & Four Coupled Axes

With Constitution true-parse coverage lifted from a median of 23.39% to 97.59%, we turned the audited analyzers into MALI13. Every evaluation split in bench/data/dev-mini/ (728 frozen rows, SHA-256 checksummed in manifest.json) is verified to have zero normalized overlap with Sebeni’s 4,340 training and distillation rows (dataset_300_samples.jsonl and dataset_10_samples.jsonl).

Rather than collapsing everything into a single Bambara-skewed accuracy, MALI13 separates four linguistic quality axes—Morphology (M), Generation Purity (G), 13-Way Language ID (L), and Morphosyntactic Cloze (C)—and pairs their macro composite MALI13-Q with equity metrics (Q_min3, the mean of each model’s three worst languages) and orthography compression metrics (Word Fertility and the Malian Diacritic Penalty over ɛ ɔ ɲ ŋ ɓ ɗ ƴ ă ə ɣ ḍ ḥ ṭ ẓ ʼ).

Key Empirical Takeaways from Edition No. 1

Frontier Lead & Tail Equity
Gemini 3.1 Pro tops the inaugural 13-model broadsheet at MALI13-Q 69.3 with the strongest worst-3 language floor (Q_min3 55.4) and 77.9 on Morphosyntactic Cloze, followed closely by Open Frontier leader glm-5.3-flash (64.5 overall, highest Morphology at 67.6 and Generation at 92.3) and Gemini 3 Flash (56.8).
The Diacritic Penalty Spread
Across all tested tokenizers, words carrying Malian IPA/orthographic diacritics (ɛ ɔ ɲ ŋ ɓ ɗ ƴ ă ə ɣ ḍ ḥ ṭ ẓ ʼ) cost 2.52× to 3.10× more subwords than plain ASCII tokens: glm-5.3-flash (2.52×, fertility 2.46) and Gemma-4 (2.57×, 2.37) fragment least, while Qwen3/SEA-LION tokenizers hit 3.10× (2.63 fertility).
2B Local 4-Bit RL vs. Cloud Giants
Running locally in 1.8 GB VRAM (6 GB RTX 3060, BPB 3.337), Sebeni-Gemma-4-E2B-GRPO-50 scores 17.6 overall—reaching 46.1 on logprob Cloze and 15.8 on zero-shot JSON Morphology (beating qwen-SEA-LION-v5-397b at 8.0 and nemotron-3-ultra-550b at 4.5), while lagging on unaligned prose Generation (8.3) and strict-JSON LID formatting (0.0).
